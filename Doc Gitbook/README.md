# Welcome to ProductReels

ProductReels lets you show short vertical videos on your store and connect them to the products in them. A shopper taps a reel, watches it full screen, sees the product card and adds the product to their cart without leaving the video.

You can use it for product demos, customer videos (UGC), tutorials, unboxings, reviews and launch teasers.

{% hint style="success" %}
**ProductReels is completely free.** There is no Pro version, no licence key and no locked features.
{% endhint %}

## A quick example

Luma Skincare is a small WooCommerce store that sells a vitamin C serum. The owner films a 20-second clip of a customer applying the serum and uploads it to ProductReels as a **reel**. They tag the serum product, so a product card with an **Add to cart** button sits under the video.

Next they create a **widget**, which is a carousel holding that reel and five others. They paste the shortcode `[productreels id="3"]` into the homepage. Visitors now see a row of vertical videos, and each one leads to a product they can buy.

The rest of this guide shows you how to do the same.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| WordPress | 6.6 |
| PHP | 7.4 |
| WooCommerce | Optional. You need it to tag products and use Add to cart. |
| Elementor | Optional. You need it only for the Elementor widget. |

{% hint style="info" %}
ProductReels works on a regular WordPress site without WooCommerce. Reels, widgets, custom buttons and statistics all work. Only product tagging and Add to cart need WooCommerce.
{% endhint %}
