# Add a reel

A reel is one vertical video plus the products or buttons that go with it. You create reels once and reuse them in as many widgets as you like.

## Open the reel sheet

You can start a new reel from three places:

* **ProductReels → Reels → Add reel**
* **ProductReels → Widgets → Add reel**
* The **Reels** step of the widget editor → **Add reel**

The reel sheet opens with a **live player preview on the left** and the reel's details on the right. The preview updates as you work, so you see the reel exactly as shoppers will.

The right side has four sections, from top to bottom.

## 1. Video

Choose where the video comes from:

| Source | What to enter | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Upload** | A file from your Media Library, or drag a video onto the sheet | Videos you own. Fastest and most private. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | One or more Shorts links | Content already on your YouTube channel |
| **Vimeo** | One or more Vimeo links | Professionally hosted brand videos |
| **Video link** | A direct link to an `.mp4` or `.webm` file | Videos on a CDN or other storage |

**Adding several at once:** in the YouTube Shorts, Vimeo and Video link tabs, paste **one link per line**.

**Example:**

```
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/AbC123xyz
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/DeF456uvw
```

A reel can hold more than one clip. The clips play in order, one after another. Use **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove** in the video list to arrange them. While an upload is running, the list shows its progress. You can keep working, but you can't save until the upload finishes.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Recommended format:** MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio, vertical 9:16 (for example 1080 × 1920). Keep clips short: 15–60 seconds works best for shopping.
{% endhint %}

## 2. Title

Give the reel a clear, descriptive title. Depending on the widget's **Look** settings, the title can appear on the thumbnail, below it, or not at all.

**Examples:**

* ✅ `Morning routine with Vitamin C serum`
* ✅ `How to layer the Linen Shirt`
* ❌ `IMG_4421.mp4`

## 3. Cover

The cover is the still image shown before the video plays.

* **Filmstrip:** scrub through the video's frames and click the one you want.
* **Upload image:** use a custom image, such as a designed thumbnail.
* **Use first frame:** quickly use the opening frame.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Can't see frames for a YouTube, Vimeo or external video?** Browsers block frame capture from videos hosted on another site. YouTube reels use YouTube's thumbnail automatically. For others, use **Upload image**.
{% endhint %}

## 4. Shop this reel

Connect the reel to what it sells. A reel needs **at least one product or button** before you can save it.

* **Tag products:** pick WooCommerce products. A product card appears in the player.
* **Add a button:** add a custom call to action with your own text and link.

See [Tag products and add buttons](tag-products-and-buttons.md) for details.

## Save the reel

Click **Save**. If something is missing, the footer tells you what. For example:

* *"Add a video to save this reel."*
* *"Add a product or button to save this reel."*
* *"Wait for the upload to finish to save this reel."*

## Real-world example

**Scenario:** Luma Skincare has a customer video of the Night Repair Cream filmed on a phone.

1. **Video →** Upload `night-cream-review.mp4`.
2. **Title →** `Real review: Night Repair Cream after 30 days`.
3. **Cover →** Scrub to the frame where the customer holds the jar to the camera.
4. **Shop this reel →** Tag *Night Repair Cream* and *Gentle Cleanser*, because both appear in the video.
5. **Save.**

In the player, shoppers now see two product cards under the video and can add either one to their cart.
