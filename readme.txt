== Description ==

**ProductReels is a free shoppable reels plugin for WooCommerce and WordPress.** Turn product videos, UGC, reviews, tutorials and unboxings into interactive video reels with product tags, product cards and add-to-cart buttons.

Display **WooCommerce reels** on product pages, homepages and landing pages as a video carousel, reel slider, grid, marquee, stacked cards or floating popup. Shoppers can swipe through fullscreen reels and explore products without leaving the video experience.

= Shoppable Reels Features =

* **Shoppable video reels:** Tag WooCommerce products, show product cards and let customers add items to the cart from a video.
* **Product page reels:** Add video carousels to product pages, including automatic placement without individual shortcodes.
* **Reels slider and carousel:** Create scrollable video reels for homepages, shop pages and landing pages.
* **Five reel layouts:** Grid, carousel, marquee, stacked cards and floating popup, plus story-style round thumbnails.
* **Floating video reels:** Show a compact product video popup after a delay, on scroll or immediately.
* **Product-specific reels:** Automatically display videos tagged to the current WooCommerce product, with fallback videos.
* **Fullscreen reels player:** Mobile swipe, mouse-wheel and keyboard navigation with video controls.
* **Video sources:** WordPress Media Library, MP4, WebM, YouTube Shorts and Vimeo.
* **Visual reel builder:** Customize appearance, spacing, overlays, product cards and responsive previews.
* **Reels analytics:** Track views, clicks, click rate, eligible paid orders and attributed revenue.
* **Easy embedding:** Reusable widgets, Elementor widget, Gutenberg block and shortcodes.
* **Lightweight loading:** Lazy-loaded videos, conditional assets and accessible player controls.

= Create WooCommerce Video Reels in 4 Steps =

1. Go to **ProductReels → Reels**, add your product video and tag WooCommerce products or a custom link.
2. Open **ProductReels → Widgets**, choose a reel layout and select videos.
3. Customize your shoppable reels using the live desktop, tablet and mobile preview.
4. Publish through Gutenberg, Elementor, a shortcode or automatic product-page placement.

Shortcode: `[productreels id="1"]`

For a specific product, use the shortcode's `product="ID"` attribute.

= Video Analytics and Performance =

Track reel views, clicks and click rate, plus eligible paid orders and revenue associated with reel-driven add-to-cart actions. Compare all-time results or the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Cancelled, refunded and failed orders are excluded from sales attribution.

Video files load as needed; widget assets only load on pages using ProductReels. The fullscreen player and YouTube/Vimeo SDKs load when required, and widget data is cached.

= Privacy and External Services =

ProductReels does not send website or visitor information to ProductReels servers. For view rate limiting, visitor IP addresses are temporarily hashed with WordPress salts and are not stored in analytics tables.

**Vimeo:** Adding a Vimeo URL may request video metadata (poster and duration) from `https://vimeo.com/api/oembed.json`, sending only the submitted video URL. Opening a Vimeo reel may load `https://player.vimeo.com/api/player.js`. Do Not Track is enabled where supported.

Vimeo Terms: https://vimeo.com/terms
Vimeo Privacy: https://vimeo.com/privacy

**YouTube:** Video thumbnails may be served from `https://img.youtube.com/vi/...`. Opening a YouTube reel may load `https://www.youtube.com/iframe_api`.

YouTube Terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
Google Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Provider SDKs are loaded only when needed. Self-hosted videos do not require these providers. Developers can disable Vimeo metadata lookup with `productreels_allow_provider_lookup`.

== Installation ==

1. Go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin** in WordPress and search for **ProductReels**.
2. Install and activate it.
3. Add videos under **ProductReels → Reels** and tag products if you use WooCommerce.
4. Create a widget, choose a layout and customize the preview.
5. Add your shoppable reels via Gutenberg, Elementor, shortcode or automatic product-page placement.

Manual installation: Upload the plugin to `/wp-content/plugins/productreels/` and activate it.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is ProductReels a free shoppable reels plugin? =

Yes. Every currently available feature is free, without a Pro version or license key.

= How do I add shoppable reels to WooCommerce product pages? =

Use automatic product-page placement, a shortcode, Gutenberg block or Elementor widget. You can display videos tagged to the current product.

= Can I add a reels slider or video carousel to my homepage? =

Yes. Choose a carousel, grid, marquee or another layout and place the widget on your homepage or landing page.

= Can I use video reels without WooCommerce? =

Yes. Video reels, galleries, custom CTAs and analytics work on standard WordPress sites. Product tagging and add-to-cart need WooCommerce.

= Do TikTok or Instagram reels import automatically? =

No direct import is available. You can upload videos you own as MP4 files, or use supported YouTube Shorts, Vimeo and hosted MP4/WebM videos.

= Can I track sales generated by shoppable videos? =

Yes. Reports include eligible paid orders and revenue linked to add-to-cart actions from reels, along with views and clicks.

= Does ProductReels work with Elementor and Gutenberg? =

Yes. Both builders are supported, along with shortcodes.

= Is the reels player mobile-friendly? =

Yes. Layouts are responsive, and the fullscreen player supports swipe navigation and accessible controls.

= What happens when I uninstall ProductReels? =

Data is preserved by default. Enable **Delete all data on uninstall** under **ProductReels → Settings** to remove plugin tables and options.
